\# Privacy Policy for Optima Zatca



\*\*Last Updated:\*\* December 2025



Optima Zatca ("the App") is an open-source ERPNext integration provided by IT Systematic.



1\. \*\*Data Collection:\*\* The App runs entirely on your own ERPNext server. We (IT Systematic) do not collect, store, or transmit your financial data, invoices, or customer information to our servers.

2\. \*\*ZATCA Integration:\*\* The App transmits data directly between your server and the ZATCA (Fatoora) portal. This transmission is governed by ZATCA's own regulations and standards.

3\. \*\*No Tracking:\*\* We do not track your usage or inject analytics code into your ERPNext instance.



For questions, please contact: support@itsystematic.com
